Exhibit 03 · The note

A letter
without an envelope.

Because some words deserve their own wall.

ADMIT TWO · ONE STORY · NO EXPIRATION
PRIVATE ADMISSION / ANNIVERSARY EDITION

Dear you,

Another year has found its way into our collection. And when I look back, I do not only see the big moments. I see all the small ones that quietly became important: the conversations that lasted too long, the laughter that came at the wrong time, the photographs we almost did not take, and the days that felt completely normal until I realised I would happily live them again.

Thank you for being part of this story. If there are more chapters ahead, I hope they are full of ordinary mornings, ridiculous jokes, unexpected adventures, and a thousand more reasons to choose you.
